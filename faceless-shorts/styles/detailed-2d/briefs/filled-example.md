# Filled example: The dropped cup

This original specification demonstrates clarity, not an approved drawing or a required story template. It does not retell a supplied video.

**Story:** A young adult attendant notices an empty brass cup has fallen beside a bench, picks it up and offers it to an older traveller. Visual-only, no voice or captions. Three beats, four seconds each, vertical 9:16. Use R7's illustrated forest language and slender outlined figures; do not copy its cast. No supernatural effects, extra characters or invented dialogue.

**C01 — attendant:** young adult man, slim, medium-brown skin, oval face with medium almond-shaped dark-brown eyes and small highlights, gently curved dark brows, a small rounded nose, and a narrow closed mouth at rest; no facial scars or marks. Short dark wavy hair, no facial hair. Plain ochre knee-length tunic, cream trousers, brown sandals. No jewelry or headwear. About one head taller than seated C02's standing equivalent. Keep this design identical across shots.

**C02 — traveller:** older adult woman, average build, warm-brown skin, rounded face, silver hair tied in a low bun. Muted green long tunic, cream scarf, dark-brown flat shoes. No jewelry. Seated throughout; calm expression changes to a small smile at the end. Exact facial details beyond those listed are ASK until the construction drawing is established.

**E01 — forest rest area:** daylight, warm light from upper screen-left. A wooden bench sits screen-right on a flat packed-earth clearing. C02 sits at its left end. Three distant tree trunks and subdued canopy behind the bench; small grass clusters along the bottom edge. Clear walking space screen-left. No palace, buildings, additional people or animals. Bench and trunk positions remain identical across cuts.

**P01 — cup:** empty, unengraved brass cup, approximately palm-sized, upright on the ground immediately screen-left of the bench. No lid, liquid or handle. C01 uses his actor-right hand. It remains in that hand at the end; offering does not imply a completed handover.

**S01, 0–4 s:** medium-wide, both people and cup visible. C01 stands screen-left facing screen-right. He looks down toward P01; eyebrows lift slightly. C02 remains seated looking toward him. Hold the discovery rather than moving every limb. End with C01's gaze on the cup. Static camera.

**S02, 4–8 s:** same side of the action, full-body crop of C01 with cup and the edge of the bench visible. C02 stays seated outside the crop. C01 bends at hips and knees, keeps both feet planted, reaches actor-right hand to the cup, closes his hand around it, then rises. The cup remains grounded until grasp contact; it then follows the hand. Do not stretch the arm or teleport the cup. This action is a capability to build and validate, not a currently available component.

**S03, 8–12 s:** medium two-person shot. C01 stands screen-left and extends the cup toward C02 at seated chest height. He looks at her face. C02 looks from the cup to him and smiles; her hands stay in her lap. End on the offer, with no transfer. Keep screen direction and wardrobe unchanged. Static camera.

**Clarification before art:** C02's detailed facial design is unresolved; ask for a selected construction-sheet design or explicit permission to choose. Do not render her affected keyframes yet. The example deliberately demonstrates a blocking question. Other independent environment planning may proceed.
