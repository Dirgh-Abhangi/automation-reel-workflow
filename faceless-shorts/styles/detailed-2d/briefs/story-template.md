# Story brief — Detailed 2D

Fill the story and the details you care about. Reuse character/location IDs; do not repeat their descriptions in every shot. Write **ASK** where you want a question, **AGENT_CHOICE** where you explicitly allow a choice, and **NONE** where something is intentionally absent. Unfilled material details will be clarified before affected keyframes. The agent may turn ordinary prose into this brief for you.

Do not use vague direction like “looks scared in a detailed palace” alone. Specify the visible emotion and relevant detail, e.g. “eyebrows raised inward, eyes looking toward the doorway, lips slightly parted; carved arch behind her, red curtain on screen-left, brass lamp on screen-right.” The agent should supply implementation coordinates from that intent, not ask you to draw the rig numerically.
