# Story Brief — Detailed 2D: Barbarika & The 3 Arrows (बर्बरीक के तीन बाण)

## 1. Story and boundaries
- **Working title:** Barbarika & The 3 Arrows (शीश का दानी — बर्बरीक की अमर परीक्षा)
- **Story in plain language:** Barbarika, the grandson of Bhima, sets off for the Kurukshetra war with only three divine arrows, bound by a vow to his mother to always support the losing side. Lord Krishna, disguised as a frail Brahmin under a sacred Peepal tree, challenges him to pierce every leaf of the tree with a single arrow while secretly stepping on one leaf. Barbarika's arrow marks every leaf and hovers above Krishna's foot. Realizing Barbarika's paradoxical vow would lead to the total annihilation of both armies, Krishna requests Barbarika's head as a divine sacrifice, which Barbarika offers willingly with a smile.
- **Exact narration/dialogue:** Hindi narration with dramatic Sanskrit mythological gravitas (approx. 75–80 seconds).
- **Era/place:** Dvapara Yuga, Kurukshetra border forest near a sacred Peepal tree.
- **Target length; aspect ratio:** ~78 seconds; vertical 9:16 (1080x1920 or 720x1280).
- **Primary visual references:** R7 (Video-74706.mp4 for forest path, Brahmin ascetic, archer, bow and arrow gestures) and R0 (Video-12446.mp4 for Kurukshetra dusk overview).
- **Must preserve:** Authentic 2D illustrated outline aesthetic, traditional attire (dhoti, angavastram, sacred thread, quiver of 3 arrows), respectful reverent tone.
- **Must never add/change:** No robotic/block geometry, no modern humor, no gore/grotesque violence; the decapitation is treated symbolically and reverently with divine light.
- **Agent may choose:** Camera framing pans, particle glow intensities, exact Hindi narration timestamps.
